Population models — side-by-side simulations

Choose a model, climate speed c, and environmental gradient b independently for simulations A and B. Defaults are c = 0.2 and b = 1. The global parameters rmax and k apply to all three models. For the sexual model, VLE is adjustable and enters both the dynamics and the physical phenotype scaling. Population scale K and the random seed appear only for stochastic asexual simulations. The two panels use synchronized snapshots and common display axes. All computations run locally in the browser through Pyodide/WebAssembly.
